Common questions

How do I connect ChatGPT, Claude or Muse?

Follow the steps in the docs. Each connection asks you to sign in and approve it once.

My AI says the file is too big to attach.

Ask it to “create an AgentTransfer upload page” — you'll get a link where you can upload a large file directly into your drive.

How do I disconnect an app?

On your account page, click Disconnect next to it. Your files stay in your drive.

How do I delete my account?

At the bottom of your account page. It removes your agents and all your files.

How do I report abuse?
