Questions

Good to know

What can a connected AI see?

Only your AgentTransfer drive and the messages sent to its own address. It can't see your other apps, chats or accounts. Disconnect it from your account page any time.

Is it free?

Yes. The free plan includes a {{.Quota}} drive, files up to {{.MaxFile}} each, and as many connected AIs as you like. Bigger plans are coming.

How big can files be?

Up to {{.MaxFile}} each. Chat apps limit what you can attach, so for big files ask your AI to "create an AgentTransfer upload page", use the drop zone on your account page, or the CLI — all three go straight to storage.

Who can download my files?

You and the AIs you connect. A file only leaves your drive when you or one of your AIs sends it, or creates a share link — and links expire within 24 hours.

Can I send files to other people?

Yes. Send to their address (name@{{.Domain}}) and it arrives in their agents' inboxes with its sha256 fingerprint. Send to any email address and they get a download link.

Can I delete my data?

Delete any file whenever you like. Deleting your account removes your connected agents and every file in your drive.

Can I run it myself?

Yes — AgentTransfer is open source. One Go binary runs the whole service on your own server, with local disk or any S3-compatible storage. GitHub.

For developers and agents

It's an MCP server and a plain HTTPS API.

Connect over MCP with OAuth at {{.MCP}}, or use an API key with the REST API (OpenAPI) and the open-source CLI. Agents can read /llms.txt.

# save a file, then send it to another agent
curl -T report.pdf {{.Base}}/v1/files/report.pdf -H "Authorization: Bearer $AGENTTRANSFER_KEY"
curl -X POST {{.Base}}/v1/send -H "Authorization: Bearer $AGENTTRANSFER_KEY" \
  -d '{"to":["dana@{{.Domain}}"],"file":"report.pdf","note":"final version"}'
